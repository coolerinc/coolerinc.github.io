SpineViewer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

SpineViewer ("the app") is a free, ad-supported Android app for previewing Spine skeletal animations, published by Pavel Shabanov ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information is processed when you use the app, why, and what choices you have.

1. Summary

  • The app has no accounts and I do not ask for your name, email or other contact details.
  • Files you open in the app (skeletons, atlases, textures) and the GIF / PNG files you export stay on your device. They are not uploaded to me or to anyone else.
  • The app uses Google services for advertising, analytics and crash reporting. These services collect device identifiers and usage data, as described below.

2. Files and data stored on your device

Imported archives are copied into the app's private storage and unpacked there. Viewer settings (selected animation, skins, background colour, camera position, export options), thumbnails and exported files are also stored locally. You can remove them at any time with "Clear Cache" in the app, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If device backup is enabled in your Android settings, Android may include part of the app's local data in your personal Google backup. This is controlled by you in the device settings and is not accessible to me.

3. Information collected through third-party services

The app includes the following Google SDKs. They collect information automatically and send it to Google:

ServiceWhat is collectedPurpose
Google AdMob Advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, ad interactions (views, clicks), diagnostic data Showing ads, limiting how often an ad is shown, measuring ad performance, fraud prevention; personalised ads only where you have allowed it
Google User Messaging Platform Your consent choices, device information needed to decide whether a consent form is required Collecting and remembering your advertising consent
Google Analytics for Firebase App instance ID, Advertising ID (where available), device model, OS version, language, country-level region, in-app events (for example: an export finished, a menu link was opened) Understanding how the app is used and improving it
Firebase Crashlytics Crash traces, device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash, an installation identifier Finding and fixing crashes
Firebase Remote Config App instance ID, app version, language, country-level region Delivering app configuration
Google Play services (in-app updates, in-app review, asset delivery) Data handled by Google Play under your Google account Updating the app, review prompts, downloading the demo file

The contents of the files you open are never part of this information.

These services are provided by Google, which acts under its own privacy terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. Advertising and your choices

The app shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may use the Advertising ID and similar identifiers to show and measure ads.

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch you are asked whether you agree to the use of your data for advertising. You can change your choice at any time in the app menu under "Privacy options". If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Android settings: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). After deletion, the identifier is no longer available to apps.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.

5. Permissions

  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) — used by the advertising and analytics SDKs.
  • Storage / media access — used only so you can pick a file to open. On current Android versions files are selected through the system file picker.
  • Internet access — used by the services listed in section 3.

6. Legal bases (EEA / UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising and the related use of device identifiers are based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described in section 4. Crash reporting, basic usage analytics and non-personalised ads are based on my legitimate interest in keeping the app working, improving it and funding its development.

7. Sharing and international transfers

I do not sell your information and I do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed in section 3. Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards.

8. Retention

I do not keep any personal data on servers of my own. Data collected by the Google services is retained by Google according to its policies and the retention settings of those services. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Because the app has no accounts, I cannot identify you from the data held by Google; the tools in section 4 are the most direct way to exercise control. You can also contact me using the address below and I will help as far as I am able.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will take the steps available to me.

11. Security

Data sent by the SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
